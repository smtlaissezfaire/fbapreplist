FBA Prep List is a static website. It does not have accounts.

The search box filters the directory in your browser. The query is not sent to us. If you share or bookmark the page, the query can sit in the address on your device.

The [list your prep center](/list-your-prep-center/) form is meant to submit to Formspree, a third-party form host, after the site owner replaces the placeholder endpoint. Until then the form does not deliver messages. Do not send passwords, payment data, or customer lists.

This site uses Google Analytics and a self-hosted analytics service to count visits and clicks. That includes which pages people open and when they follow a link to a prep center's website, phone number, or email. Google Analytics may set a cookie in your browser. The self-hosted service runs at analytics.smtlaissezfaire.com and records the same kind of visit and click counts. The site does not use advertising cookies and does not sell this information.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep ordinary server logs. For a correction or a privacy question, use the listing form once it is connected.
